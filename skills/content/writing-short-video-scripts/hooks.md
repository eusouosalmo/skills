# Hooks

Five patterns for the first 3 seconds of an educational technical video, each tied to the mechanism behind it. The examples all start from the same lukewarm claim, "testar o código é importante", and show form only; their facts are made up.

| Pattern | Why it holds | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The viewer's situation plus an unexpected consequence | people attend to what is about them; a broken expectation pulls them to find out why | "Se tu só testa o código clicando na tela, tu testa o caminho que já sabe que funciona." |
| A bet | asking for a guess raises curiosity about the answer | "Quantos bugs um teste de dez linhas pegou no meu último projeto? Eu chutei zero." |
| A common belief contradicted by a concrete detail | broken expectation plus something specific to picture | "O teste que mais me salvou não testava regra de negócio nenhuma. Ele só abria a tela de login." |
| The author's scene at the turning point, outcome open | an unfinished sequence the viewer wants to see end | "Sexta, cinco da tarde, eu subi uma correção de uma linha. Às seis, ninguém conseguia pagar no site." |
| Calling the audience by role, with a condition | people recognise themselves and self-select | "Se tu é desenvolvedor e já ouviu 'em produção funcionava', esse vídeo é sobre o teste que faltou." |

## The five questions

A hook passes when it answers all five:

1. **Who?** The viewer or the author is in the sentence, not only a process, an area or "eles".
2. **What concrete thing?** A number, a scene, a tool, a moment.
3. **What question stays open?** After the line, the viewer wants to know something. A line that already states the conclusion leaves nothing open.
4. **What does it contradict?** Something the viewer believed before.
5. **Is it true?** A contradiction keeps the qualifier that makes it true ("teste demais atrasa o projeto *quando testa a mesma camada duas vezes*"), and the video delivers what the hook promises. A hook the video does not pay off is empty clickbait.

The lukewarm claim "testar o código é importante" fails the first four: no one is in it, nothing is concrete, it states its own conclusion, and every viewer already agrees.

Write two or three hooks from different patterns and use the strongest as the script's hook; the others go to `Ganchos alternativos`.

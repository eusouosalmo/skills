# Sources

No text in this skill is copied from a third party. The method is described in this skill's own words.

- **Temas & Roteiros** (Bárbara Torres), lesson on short video scripts: credit for the five-step order the spine follows (attention in the first seconds, short context, the story, connection with the viewer, CTA), and lesson on checking information before publishing. The course material is not reproduced here.
- **Hook, Story, Offer** (Russell Brunson): the same sequence in three blocks; the offer becomes a CTA that asks for a comment, since there is nothing to sell.
- **Research in this repo**: `docs/research/roteiro-de-video.md`. One ask per CTA (Hormozi, section 7.1); the niche in the hook so the right viewer recognises the video as theirs (Kallaway, section 3.7); writing for the ear and the audio-only pass (sections 6.1 and 6.7); 130 words per minute for Brazilian Portuguese speech (section 6.4); the tension between a spoken follow request and the loop of a short video (sections 5.1 and 9).
- **Research on the psychology of the script** in this repo: `docs/research/psicologia-do-roteiro-curto.md`. Curiosity gap, prediction, broken expectation, self-reference and concreteness behind the hook patterns and their four questions (section 2); the chain test, the reason with content and Chekhov's gun (section 3, with Langer 1978 on reasons); causal and contrast connectives over additive ones (section 4, Kleijn, Pander Maat and Sanders 2019); keeping the author's story at the degree of the notes (section 5); telling it as past experience with no date or employer (section 7).
- **Design**: issue #9 of eusouosalmo/skills (scope, spine, CTA, output in two parts).
- **Observed failures**: the corrections eusouosalmo made by hand to two Instagram reels about software architecture, which became the eval scenarios in `evals/evals.json` and the items of `checklist.md`.

# Checklist

Objective items only, read at the end. Style is the user's call.

## Checked by scripts/check-voice.py

- The pronoun matches the register: "tu" in spoken, "você" in written.
- No catchphrase in the written register.
- No filler word in the spoken register.
- No emoji, em dash, en dash, unicode arrow or ellipsis character.
- No expression that gives away AI. The list is [assets/ai-tells.tsv](assets/ai-tells.tsv), built only from failures seen in real drafts. A new one goes there, with the draft it came from.

## Checked by you

- Every fact, number, reason and result is in the input. Each gap is a `[falta: ...]`.
- No swearing.
- No hype or guru phrase: a promise of results ("vai mudar tua carreira"), a secret, a formula.
- Every "você" left in spoken text is impersonal.

# Sources

The full research, with quotes, dates and the strength of each finding, is in the repository's `docs/research/legenda-de-post.md` (2026-10-06). This file lists what backs each rule of the skill. **Confirmed**: platform, law or published study. **Report**: guide or practitioner. **Inference**: a conclusion with no source that states it.

| Rule | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| The caption adds context, it does not retell | TikTok Creator Academy, [Top content strategy tips](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/developing-your-content-strategy): "Use the space in your captions to add context and share more about a video" | Confirmed (TikTok); applied to the other platforms by inference |
| First line names the topic in search words | Instagram, [How Instagram Search works](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works) (2021); TikTok Creator Academy, [Getting discovered with Search](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/Getting-discovered-with-Search) (2026); YouTube Help, [Tips for video descriptions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449) | Confirmed |
| First line does not copy the hook nor give the answer | Loewenstein (1994), information gap, in `docs/research/psicologia-do-roteiro-curto.md` 2.1; preference of eusouosalmo (correction 40) | Inference from a confirmed mechanism |
| About 125, 100 to 150 and 140 characters before "mais" | Third-party guides; no official page | Report |
| One ask per caption | de Vries et al. (2012); Chae (2021): "multiple calls to action within one message decrease engagement"; Eppler and Bünzli (2025) | Confirmed (brand and nonprofit posts) |
| No ask for a word, tag, share or like | Meta, [Engagement bait](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait); Instagram video of 2024 (via Social Media Today); LinkedIn feed updates of 2022 and 2026 | Confirmed |
| Asking to like, comment or subscribe is fine on YouTube | YouTube Help, [Spam, deceptive practices and scams policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973) | Confirmed |
| Follow ask goes in the caption, not in the speech | `docs/research/roteiro-de-video.md` 9 (loop of short videos); no platform compares the two | Inference |
| The fact, not where it was checked | Mayer, coherence principle, in `docs/research/psicologia-do-roteiro-curto.md` 3.3; preference of eusouosalmo, who finds it unusual in captions (correction 41) | Confirmed in multimedia learning, not tested on captions; the rest is preference |
| Links not clickable; "link na bio" | YouTube Help, [Sharing links](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639) (Shorts, since 2023); Meta statement on the 2026 paid test; Mosseri (2025): saying "link in bio" does not cut reach | Confirmed; TikTok is report |
| @ notifies and links to the profile | Instagram API, [IG User Media](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media); LinkedIn Help | Confirmed (notification); link is observed use |
| Hashtags: specific, at most 5 on Instagram, 3 shown on YouTube | @creators announcement (Dec 2025, via Social Media Today); YouTube Help, [Hashtags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658); TikTok Search article | Confirmed |
| Hashtags do not raise reach on Instagram | Mosseri (2025, via Social Media Today) | Confirmed with caveat |
| `#publi` before "mais" for commissioned links | CONAR, Guia de Publicidade por Influenciadores Digitais (2026); CDC art. 36 | Confirmed |
| Source credit with author and work | Lei 9.610/1998, art. 46, III | Confirmed for quotes; good practice otherwise |
| Series promise brings followers | No data found; Ovsiankina (tendency to resume), Ghibellini and Meier (2025) | No evidence |

# Sources

- **Pipeline and the community baseline** (auto-editor, the vincentventalon rough-cut skill and others: silence by audio level, retake by text similarity, last take kept, margins of 0.09 to 0.5 s): `docs/research/edicao-de-video-com-ia.md`, sections 2 and 3.3, in this repo.
- **Approval before render:** agents in Timeline-Bench (2026 preprint, 43 human editors) pass the technical checks and fail on quality, and claim success in most runs; the research note, section 3.2. The takes table is where the author's judgement enters before the render.
- **Margins** (every pause above about 0.35 s down to about 0.2 s, about 0.1 s each side): measured by silencedetect on the author's Jev project (2026-10-15), not by Whisper word times, which stretch over pauses. At the author's cuts almost no silence is left at the edge; the author cuts pauses of 0.3 to 0.6 s by eye and keeps others of the same length, with no boundary that separates them (sentence end or not). Cutting every pause above 0.35 s to about 0.18 s gave 134 s against the author's 133 s with 96% in common, where the earlier rule (only pauses above 0.5 s, 0.35 s left) gave 140 s. Close to the 250 ms breath pause Rubin et al. 2013 put back between sentences (research note, section 3.4).
- **Recording order and no reordering:** in the same four projects no kept piece is out of the raw clips' order.
- **Register of the take:** the spoken register of eusouosalmo uses "tu" (`CONTEXT.md`, Voice and Register). In the WhatsApp reel the author kept the "tu"/"teu" takes where the baseline picked the "você"/"seu" ones.
- **The last take is not always the one:** in the same reel the author kept the first take of a line whose second take starts right after "não, deixa, vou gravar de novo".
- **Memory cap:** a single `filter_complex` with 63 trims passed 11 GB and froze a 16 GB WSL (2026-10-15). `scripts/run_capped.sh` sizes the cap to the free memory (70%, leaving 2 GB) and uses a systemd-run scope, tested to kill only the capped process; macOS and Windows run without it.
- **Register over wording, whole takes** (verdict of eusouosalmo on 2026-10-15, after the first run with the skill): in the WhatsApp reel the author kept "se tu mandar essa mensagem" over "se você mandar essa senha" and the whole "tu" take with "com ela com o texto" over a clean "seu" take; a splice of two takes inside a line stays an alternative, never the default. Searched the research note (sections 3.3 and 3.6) for evidence on mid-sentence jump cuts in talking-head video and found none, so the splice rule is the author's preference, with the reason that the cut lands mid-sentence on the face.
- **Warm-up out, open on the hook:** the first one to three seconds and the cold open, `docs/research/roteiro-de-video.md`, sections 3.1 to 3.9, in this repo. In the pyramid reel the author cut "Bora trocar um papo sobre experiência" and opened on the hook; the baseline kept it.
- **No rule for lone connectors:** an earlier count said the author cut lone 'e', 'então', 'porque' at the start of phrases; checked against the author's final captions (2026-10-15), those words are all in the published reel. The count came from Whisper placing a word inside the pause cut next to it. A rule built on it made the skill cut 'então pensa na pirâmide', which the author kept, so the skill has no hesitation status.

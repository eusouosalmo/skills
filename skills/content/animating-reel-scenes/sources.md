# Sources

What backs each rule of the skill. **Observed**: tested by eusouosalmo in a real reel (the Jev reel, 2026-10-07). **Design system**: the author's own video and motion guidelines, read at run time. **Preference**: eusouosalmo's call, with where the search for a source went.

| Rule | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Alpha overlay as QuickTime Animation (.mov qtrle) only | Jev reel: the qtrle `.mov` kept its transparency in CapCut, the VP9 `.webm` with alpha came out black; ProRes 4444 untested there and 90 to 340 MB for 32 s | Observed (one editor, one machine) |
| Ask the author to test the `.mov` in CapCut first | Same test: alpha support varies by editor and version | Observed |
| Caveats and CTA as stickers over the face, not full scenes | eusouosalmo during the Jev reel: "deixa eu aparecendo um pouco mais [...] as coisas precisam aparecer mais em explicações" | Preference; no study found comparing face time with motion in explainer reels (searched `docs/research/roteiro-de-video.md` and `psicologia-do-roteiro-curto.md`) |
| Hook on the face | Same preference, and the approved Jev plan | Preference |
| One concept per scene: split parts that ask for different reading paces | eusouosalmo asked to split a Jev scene that held a quick list of use cases and a slow code reading ("divide") | Preference |
| Overlay in the band above the head, caption zone clear | Approved Jev overlay (y 300 to 730); CapCut captions over the lower frame | Observed |
| Timecode `HH:MM:SS:FF` at 30fps | CapCut timeline display at 30fps | Observed |
| Safe margins, stamp entrance, dry cuts, colour by role, typing pace | The author's design system guidelines (video and motion) | Design system |
| Third-party footage and covers: ask whether the author can use them | Lei 9.610/98, art. 46 ([summary by BMA](https://www.bmalaw.com.br/conteudo/direito-propriedade-intelectual/artigos-e-noticias/excecoes-aos-direitos-autorais-quais-sao-os-limites-para-as-citacoes/pdf)): small excerpts are allowed only when the reproduction is not the main point of the new work and does not harm the normal use of the original | Confirmed (law); whether a given clip fits is the author's call, not the skill's |
| Real people: ask, never generate | Código Civil, art. 20, and STJ Súmula 403 ([summary](https://legale.com.br/blog/responsabilidade-civil-imagem-na-publicidade-e-sumula-403/)): using someone's image for commercial purposes without authorisation is unlawful, damage presumed; Instagram requires the "AI info" label on realistic generated people ([Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/761121959519495)) | Confirmed |
| Proof from the author, facts from the primary source | Jev reel: the DOOM demo was the author's clip of Jev playing ("Esse aí é na verdade o Jev rodando junto com DOOM"); the baseline without the skill drew a stand-in and did not check the numbers | Observed |
| No caption strip inside scenes | The reel's captions are added over everything in CapCut; the baseline put a caption strip inside a scene and warned it would duplicate | Observed |
| Stamp the term to keep, not the reasoning | Schneider et al. (2018), signaling meta-analysis: retention g = 0.53, transfer g = 0.33; Mayer and Johnson (2008): short labels raised retention, not transfer. In `docs/research/texto-na-tela.md` 4.1 and 2.2 | Confirmed (university students in multimedia lessons); applied to reels by inference |
| Rare highlights, one at a time | Lorch, Lorch and Klusewitz (1995): heavy cueing performed like no cueing; Hunt (1995) on the isolation effect. `texto-na-tela.md` 4.3 | Confirmed; the "one per caveat" count is inference |
| One to three words from the speech, on the spoken frame | Mayer and Johnson (2008); Adesope and Nesbit (2012); temporal contiguity in Mayer and Fiorella (2014), 9 of 9 tests. `texto-na-tela.md` 2.2, 4.5, 5.3 | Confirmed for the mechanism; the word limit is inference |
| No whole spoken sentence beside the caption | Mayer and Fiorella (2014), redundancy, 16 of 16 tests, median d = 0.86; Kalyuga, Chandler and Sweller (2004); Kruger, Hefer and Matthew (2014): with subtitles, gaze split about 39% face, 43% subtitles. `texto-na-tela.md` 2.1, 2.4 | Confirmed (mechanism), inference for reels; eusouosalmo agreed ("frase não é muito comum") |
| Sentence in a scene: slightly shortened, close to the speech | [Yue, Bjork and Bjork (2013)](https://sites.lifesci.ucla.edu/psych-bjorklab/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2016/07/YueBjorkBjork2013_redundancy.pdf): "an advantage for a small amount of discrepancy", "too much discrepancy resulted in learning just as poor as [...] identical"; learners preferred identical text anyway. `texto-na-tela.md` 3.1, 3.2 | Confirmed (one lab, two experiments) |
| Always wait for stills approval | eusouosalmo, 2026-10-07: "espera sempre minha aprovação dos stills antes de renderizar"; with the skill, two runs rendered early when asked for the finished file | Preference, observed failure |
| Fixed layout: `cenas/`, `stills/`, `exports/`, `timeline.md`, numbered by timeline order | eusouosalmo, 2026-10-07, asked where the exported videos end up; runs without a layout saved them in four different folders | Preference, observed failure; no source on folder layout (searched `docs/research/`), an organisational choice |
| Ask for a CapCut screenshot with the caption on, not only a frame | eusouosalmo sent one for the pyramid reel (2026-10-07): it showed the caption at about 74 to 82% of the height, in large type, with keywords already in yellow, none of which a raw frame shows | Observed |
| Facts: open the primary source; coverage that disagrees is not enough | With the skill, a run searched only coverage, missed "40x-200x" in the company's own post and told the author the speech was wrong | Observed failure |
| Check prerequisites with `scripts/setup.sh` before rendering | eusouosalmo asked for it (2026-10-07); in this session Pillow could not be installed globally (PEP 668), and Playwright's Chromium needs system libraries on Linux ([Playwright docs: install --with-deps](https://playwright.dev/python/docs/browsers#install-system-dependencies)) | Preference, observed failure |
| Time every word with Whisper; text from the SRT | Pyramid reel: the estimate put "pirâmide" at 11.81 s, while the author's CapCut screenshot shows it highlighted at 12.4 s (about 18 frames early). [faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) word timestamps; in a test on a 73 s reel, `small` on CPU took 23 s and misheard names ("Cloud" for "Claude"), which the SRT text fixes | Observed |
| Questions as multiple choice, recommended option first | eusouosalmo asked for it (2026-10-07); a reference reel shows the same gate ("1. Sim, renderizar 2. Não, quero ajustar") before any render | Preference |
| Style file from references and verdicts | A reference reel's main point: "a skill sozinha traz um vídeo genérico", fixed by a style file written from 4 liked reels and the corrections; matches this repo's rule that lessons are reasons | Report (one creator) and preference |
| Split screen for long explanations | eusouosalmo asked for it after a reference reel; the only study found ([iMotions, exploratory](https://imotions.com/blog/publications/short-form-videos-an-exploratory-study-on-the-impact-of-subtitles-and-asmr-split-screen-format-options-using-eyegaze-and-facial-expression-data/)) measured more emotional engagement and no difference in recall; the "+38% engagement" figures in circulation have no traceable source | Preference; evidence weak |
| Source shot of the primary source | A reference reel shows the cited documentation with the sentence highlighted in time with the speech; extends the rule that facts come from the primary source. Quoting a sentence to support what is said fits Lei 9.610/98 art. 46 | Report and law |
| A sound for every motion event, synthesised, under the speech | eusouosalmo after the arquitetura-desce reel (2026-10-08): "mais sound effects conforme as animações acontecem, objetos aparecem na tela e durante o aparecimento e sumiço das telas", after 9 events in 81 s felt thin; a reference reel times a sound to each event ("sfx('thud', 3.74)"); synthesising avoids licensing samples; the first track peaked at -4 dB against speech at -3.6 dB, so the ceiling went to about -10 dB | Preference, report and observed |
| Edit suggestions as a list, transitions only if the style file allows | Zoom, black and white, glitch and wipe in a reference reel are cuts on the recording, done in the editor; the author's design system asks for dry cuts | Report and design system |
| Split screen: deliver the top panel with alpha by default; compose only from a caption-free recording | arquitetura-desce reel (2026-10-07): the recording came with burned-in captions, and the composed crop carried them to about 1680 px, under the platform's bar; eusouosalmo then said they move the face down and place the caption themselves in CapCut | Observed and preference |
| Centre the drawing in the split's top panel, at least 170 px from the top | eusouosalmo (2026-10-07): the drawing pushed down by the 250 px margin read as misaligned. The 14% top margin circulates as Meta's Reels ad spec, with no published figure for organic reels ([summary](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/)); Meta's own page not reached | Preference; source secondary |

# Sources

No text in this skill is copied from a third party. The examples are eusouosalmo's own speech from published videos.

- **Voice material**: the pilot video on YouTube, the published Instagram reels and their scripts. The inventory and what may be public are in issue #5; the voice and register model, scope and files are in issue #6, with the amendment from issue #9.
- **Temas & Roteiros** (Bárbara Torres), lesson on using ChatGPT: credit for the idea of replacing expressions that give away AI-written text. The course's own list is not used; [assets/ai-tells.tsv](assets/ai-tells.tsv) holds only expressions seen in drafts written on the user's real material.
- **Skill authoring best practices** (Anthropic): https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/agent-skills/best-practices. Evaluations before the skill, three scenarios from observed failures, a checklist read at the end, scripts for what code can check.
- **Agent Skills specification**: https://agentskills.io/specification. Frontmatter, `compatibility`, the `scripts/` and `assets/` folders.

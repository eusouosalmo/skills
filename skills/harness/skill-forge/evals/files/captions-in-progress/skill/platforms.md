# Platforms

What changes from one platform to another. Sources and the strength of each rule are in [sources.md](sources.md).

| | Instagram Reels | TikTok | YouTube Shorts | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What shows first | First line, about 125 characters before "mais" | About 100 to 150 characters | The title in the player; the description sits in a menu | About 140 characters on mobile before "ver mais" |
| First line | Topic in search words, plus an open question | Same as Instagram | The title (up to 100 characters) does the hook's job with the 1 or 2 main words; the description's first lines describe the video | The text is the post: open with the scene or question in the voice skill's written register, then the topic |
| Links | Not clickable: "link na bio" | Not clickable: "link na bio" | Not clickable in the description: use the related video link or the channel links | Clickable; the post stands without it |
| Hashtags | At most 5 | Few; 3 to 5 | Up to 3 show above the title; more than 60 voids all | Optional; 0 to 3 |
| Asks to avoid | A specific word, a tag, "compartilha se", "curte se" | Like-for-like, false incentive to follow | Rewards for likes or subscribers; asking to like, comment or subscribe is fine | "Comenta SIM se concorda" and other bait |
| Register | The voice skill decides by channel | The voice skill decides | The voice skill decides | Written ("você") |

- **Series**: Instagram can link Reels in a series ("link a reel"), and Shorts can link a related video. Mention the feature to the author when the video is part of a series.
- **Advertising**: when the link earns a commission, or the video was paid for or given, `#publi` or `#publicidade` (or the platform's paid partnership label) goes in the part visible before "mais". Tagging the brand is not enough (CONAR guide, 2026).
- **Mentions**: an @ notifies the person on Instagram and LinkedIn. Mention someone only when they are part of the content.
